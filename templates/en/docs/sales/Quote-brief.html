11 · Ownership

  • To the client, on payment of each phase: source code, repositories, database and schema, APIs, configuration, documentation, design assets.
  • Accounts in the client's name from day one: domain, hosting, database, third-party services (and, with apps, Apple Developer and Google Play). The client opens them — with our guidance — and invites us as administrators, and can remove us at any time without losing anything.
  • Third-party libraries and services stay under their own licences: we list them in an annex, with licence and cost, so it is clear what is transferred and what is not.
  • Free exit: at the end we hand over access, deployment and restore documentation, and one day of handover to whoever takes over.
