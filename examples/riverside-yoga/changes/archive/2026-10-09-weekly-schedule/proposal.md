## Why

Today the timetable is a picture posted on Instagram every Sunday. Members
need one place, always up to date, that shows what is on and how full it is.

## What changes

- A public schedule page, server-rendered and indexable, with the current and
  next week.
- Spots left on every session, «full» when there are none.
- Filters by class type, level and instructor.

## Specs touched

- `specs/schedule.md`: new module.

## Out of this change

- Booking from the schedule: `booking-rules`.
- Class and instructor detail pages: they come with the content from the
  studio, in the same estimate item, as a small follow-up.
