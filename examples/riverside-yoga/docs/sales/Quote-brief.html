1 · In short

Riverside Yoga wants to stop running bookings on WhatsApp and class packs on a spreadsheet. Members will see the weekly schedule, book and cancel from their phone, and pay online for drop-ins and class packs; instructors tick attendance; the front desk manages timetable, members, credits and refunds. The late-cancellation rule (free up to 12 hours before, then the credit is lost) is what stops the money lost on no-shows.

We propose two releases. Launch, in about six weeks, puts schedule, bookings and class packs online before the January rush, and fits the client's budget on its own. Memberships, three weeks later, adds monthly subscriptions, the automatic waiting list and the reports.

3 · How we build it

One mobile-first web application, one managed database in Europe, and Stripe for every payment. No app store, no server to run: members book from the phone's browser, and the studio pays a few tens of euros a month to keep it online. The booking rules — capacity, the 12-hour window, credits — are enforced in the database, so two people can never take the last spot.

11 · Ownership

  • To the client, on payment of each phase: source code, repository, database and schema, configuration, documentation, design assets.
  • Accounts in the studio's name from day one: domain, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, email, monitoring. The studio opens them — with our guidance — and invites us as administrators, and can remove us at any time without losing anything.
  • Third-party libraries and services stay under their own licences: we list them in an annex, with licence and cost.
  • Free exit: at the end we hand over access, deployment and restore documentation, and one day of handover to whoever takes over.

12 · Assumptions, exclusions, open points

To clarify at the meeting

PointWhy it matters
Do class packs expire?We assume packs of 5 expire after 2 months and packs of 10 after 4. No expiry is simpler; expiry with extensions by the front desk is included.
Existing credits on the spreadsheetWe include a one-off import of members and remaining credits from a clean spreadsheet. Cleaning it is the studio's job.
RefundsWe assume refunds go back to the card through Stripe. Store credit instead of refunds is a setting, not extra work.
Memberships and waiting list fairnessDoes a member with an unlimited membership take a waiting-list spot before a pack holder? We assume first come, first served.
VAT and invoicesStripe receipts are included. If the studio needs tax invoices from its accounting software, that integration is not.

Not included

  • Third-party service costs (§ 7), accounts and licences: in the studio's name and paid by the studio.
  • Content: photos, class descriptions, instructor bios, translations.
  • Legal advice: privacy notice, terms and the cancellation policy text are written by the studio's lawyer; we provide the technical information.
  • Marketing, campaigns, user acquisition.
  • Native mobile app (§ 6).
