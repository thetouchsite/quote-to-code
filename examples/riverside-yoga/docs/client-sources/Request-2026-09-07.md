Subject: Quote request – booking website for our yoga studio

Hello,

we run Riverside Yoga, a studio with two rooms and eight instructors, about
350 active members. Today people book by WhatsApp and pay cash or bank
transfer, and we track class packs in a spreadsheet. It does not scale and we
lose money on no-shows.

We would like a website where people can:

1. see the weekly schedule (class type, level, instructor, room, spots left);
2. create an account and book or cancel a class — cancellations are free up to
   12 hours before, later the credit is lost;
3. buy a drop-in class or a pack of 5 or 10 classes online by card;
4. later, subscribe to a monthly unlimited membership;
5. join a waiting list when a class is full and get the spot automatically if
   someone cancels.

Instructors should see their classes and tick attendance from their phone.
We, at the front desk, need to manage the timetable (most classes repeat every
week), instructors, members, credits and refunds, and see attendance and
revenue per month.

Members should get an email confirmation and a reminder the day before.

It must work well on phones: most of our members will book from there. A
native app could come later.

We would love to have the booking and payments live before the January
rush. Our budget is roughly 20,000–30,000 euros for the first version.

Could you send us an estimate with costs, timeline and running costs?

Thank you,
Anna Lindqvist
Riverside Yoga
